About Horizon3 Labs

Built to move AI from idea to impact.

Horizon3 Labs is Unilever's global AI innovation hub in Toronto. H3L brings together technical, product, design, and partnership talent to explore new opportunities, shape working prototypes, and help strong ideas travel across the business.

Where H3L sits The lab is part of Unilever's broader data, analytics, and AI ecosystem. It works with business teams, external partners, and Canada's AI community to turn emerging technology into practical solutions.

History

A lab for the next horizon.

H3L was created to give Unilever a focused place to explore what comes next with AI: not only research, and not only demos, but useful work that can become part of how the business operates.

2023

Launched in Toronto

Unilever opened Horizon3 Labs in one of the world's strongest AI ecosystems, creating a dedicated global hub for AI innovation.

Hybrid model

Built for collaboration

The lab works across Unilever teams and with academics, startups, and industry partners so ideas can be tested from more than one angle.

Now

Focused on scale

H3L turns promising concepts into prototypes, workflows, and reusable patterns that can support real business needs.

How we work

Small teams, practical outcomes.

H3L works best when a clear business problem meets fast technical exploration. The goal is to learn quickly, make the work visible, and move the strongest ideas toward adoption.

Discover

Frame the right problem

Clarify the audience, workflow, data, and decision that the project needs to support.

Prototype

Make the idea tangible

Build enough of the experience to test the value, not just explain it in a slide.

Validate

Pressure-test with users

Use feedback, examples, and real constraints to sharpen what should move forward.

Scale

Share what can travel

Turn strong patterns into reusable assets, stories, and workflows for the wider business.
